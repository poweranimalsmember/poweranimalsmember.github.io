Purpose and availability

GarageMath Sync is a private utility operated for the owner's GarageMath workspace. This website is informational; it is not a public synchronization service, software download, account-registration service or public research database.

Authorized files and accounts

Use the utility only with accounts and files you own or have permission to access. Authorizing Google Drive access does not grant rights to third-party content. Google Drive and GitHub remain subject to their respective providers' terms.

Verification and operational limits

Keep independent backups, review conflicts and validate the initial baseline before any production use. The current installation is restricted to isolated test directories. Unattended production operation requires a separate acceptance and activation decision. The utility's configuration is intended to preserve existing divergent files and avoid automatic deletion; that design is not a guarantee against every failure or interruption.

No service guarantee

No uptime, support-response time, perfect-replica status or uninterrupted authorization is promised. Google or network restrictions may interrupt access. Research results and compatibility rules require their own validation; file synchronization does not verify their substantive correctness.

Privacy and support

The privacy policy explains how authorization and file information are handled. Application questions can be directed to the support contact displayed in Google's GarageMath Sync consent screen. Do not send passwords, authorization tokens or private files through public channels.
